# VendorDesk — 30-Day GTM Playbook

**The truth:** the app is built. Your only job now is distribution. No
network? Cold outreach IS your network. 20–30 personalized DMs a day,
every day, is the entire strategy.

---

## Week 1 — Setup + Pilot prep (Days 1–7)

- [ ] Follow `README.md`: Supabase → deploy → verify checklist passes.
- [ ] Buy the domain, attach to Vercel.
- [ ] Build **your own demo shop**: "Mama Chidinma Kitchen" with 6 real
      food items and nice photos. This is your sales weapon.
- [ ] Record a 30–45s screen recording on your phone: open the store → add
      jollof → checkout → WhatsApp message arrives. Post it on X, TikTok,
      IG, and your WhatsApp status.
- [ ] Create an outreach tracker (Google Sheet): `handle | platform | date
      DM'd | replied? | call? | pilot? | paid? | notes`.
- [ ] Identify 100 target vendors: IG food vendors, boutique/thrift sellers,
      cake makers, phone accessories. **Filter: active, taking orders in
      DM/comments ("DM to order", "price in DM").**

## Week 2 — Pilot wave (Days 8–14)

- [ ] Start the DM cadence: **25/day** (script below). Expect ~5–8 replies,
      ~2–3 demos per 25.
- [ ] Offer the first **10 vendors a free 30-day pilot** (no card). Set up
      their catalog FOR them during the demo call — this is the wow moment.
- [ ] Ask every pilot for one thing: *"When you get your first order through
      it, screenshot it and send me."*
- [ ] Join 10+ local "buy & sell"/vendor WhatsApp groups and FB marketplace
      groups. Share the demo video there (as value, not spam).

## Week 3 — First money (Days 15–21)

- [ ] Day 15: message every active pilot: *"Your free month ends [date].
      ₦3,500/month, or ₦35,000/year — most are choosing yearly. Want me to
      activate it?"* Take payment via your own Paystack link.
- [ ] Offer **Done-For-You setup** to vendors who liked it but are busy:
      ₦40k–₦80k (you build the catalog, brand their page, 15-min training).
      This is your fastest cash — one sale = 11 months of subscriptions.
- [ ] Launch the referral loop: *"Refer a vendor, get 1 month free when they
      pay."*

## Week 4 — Systematize (Days 22–30)

- [ ] Keep the 25 DMs/day. Track and improve the script based on objections.
- [ ] Repost one customer win every day (order screenshots are gold).
- [ ] Day 30 review: DMs → replies → demos → pilots → paid. If reply rate
      < 10%, the script or targeting is wrong — fix targeting first.

## The DM script (send as-is, personalize the bracket)

**First message (IG DM / WhatsApp):**

> Hey [shop name]! 👋 I saw your [jollof/bcakes/thrift] page — the [specific
> item] looks great. Quick one: when people message you "how much?" at
> 2am while you're asleep, do you lose those orders?
>
> I built a tool that gives you one link — full menu with photos and prices,
> customers pick what they want, and the order lands in your WhatsApp as a
> tidy message. Works 24/7 even while you sleep.
>
> I'm onboarding 10 vendors free this month — want me to set yours up? It
> takes 15 minutes together.

**Why it works:** compliments first, pain second (missed orders = real
money), proof of scarcity, and you do the work.

**Objection → response:**

| Objection | Response |
|---|---|
| "How much?" | "Free for your first month, then ₦3,500/month — less than one delivery fee. If it doesn't bring you orders, don't pay." |
| "I just use WhatsApp status" | "Exactly — this link goes IN your status and bio. Status disappears in 24hrs; your link stays in your bio forever." |
| "Let me think about it" | "No wahala. Can I set it up free anyway so you can see your own shop live? If you don't like it, I delete it." |
| "Is it safe? I don't trust apps" | "Fair. I'll build it in front of you on a call, you keep the login, and I'm [your name] — here's my number and the demo shop." |

## Money math (be honest with yourself)

- 25 DMs/day × 6 days = 150/week → at a 12% reply rate ≈ 18 conversations
  → ~6 demos → ~3 pilots per week.
- 10 pilots after 3 weeks → 4–5 convert at ₦3,500 = **₦14k–₦17.5k MRR**
  → plus 3–4 DFY setups = **₦160k–₦280k one-time**.
- Every DFY setup sold is also a monthly subscriber for month 2+.
- Realistic month-1 total: **₦180k–₦300k** — the budget you started with,
  earned back before the reserve is touched.

## Non-negotiables

1. **The demo shop must be beautiful.** Vendors buy what they see.
2. **Do the setup for them on the call.** Friction kills conversion.
3. **Follow up 2×** — most sales happen on the 2nd or 3rd message.
4. **Screenshot every win** and post it. Social proof compounds.
